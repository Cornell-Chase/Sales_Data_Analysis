Insights¶

  1. The data shows that customers spend a lot more on technology than any other category.
  2. Customers from the west make the most purchases followed by the east.
  3. Office supplies are responsible for the biggest profit margins.
  4. Mobile phone purchases contribute the largest in terms of sales followed closely by chairs.
  5. Customers from the south make the least of purchases.

Deductions And Recommendations¶

  1. Business owners should invest in research and marketing in the low sale perfoming regions to identify shortfalls and improve upon sales.
  2. Low sale products like fasteners and labels could have promotional prices to boost sales.
  3. Data on dates and time should be collected to be able to make decisions on seasonal products.
